# Privacy Policy — WolfPass

**Last updated: September 23, 2026**

WolfPass ("the App") is developed by Rafael Tessmann.

This Privacy Policy explains how WolfPass handles user and device information.

## 1. Information stored by WolfPass

WolfPass is a password manager designed to store passwords and related information locally on the user's device.

The following information may be stored locally in the App:

* Password names;
* Usernames;
* Passwords;
* Notes;
* Password usage information, such as copy counts;
* Creation and update dates.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers.

WolfPass does not currently require users to create an account or provide an email address, telephone number, or other account credentials.

## 2. Password information

Passwords and other credentials stored in WolfPass are intended to remain on the user's device.

WolfPass does not transmit stored passwords to the developer.

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their device and for protecting their device from unauthorized access.

## 3. Advertising

WolfPass uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share certain information from the user's device, including:

* IP address and approximate location;
* App interactions;
* Diagnostic information;
* Device and other identifiers.

This information may be used for purposes including:

* Advertising and marketing;
* Analytics;
* Security, compliance, and fraud prevention.

Google may process this information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Premium purchases

WolfPass may offer Premium features through a one-time in-app purchase provided through Google Play.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. WolfPass does not receive or store the user's complete payment card information.

Information related to purchases may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data sharing

WolfPass does not sell users' stored passwords or personal information.

Information collected by third-party services integrated into the App, such as Google Mobile Ads, may be transmitted to and shared with those third parties as described in this Privacy Policy and their applicable privacy policies.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by WolfPass remains on the user's device until the user deletes it through the App, deletes the relevant entries, clears the App's data, or uninstalls the App.

Because WolfPass does not currently maintain user accounts or a developer-operated server containing users' passwords, the developer does not maintain a separate copy of passwords stored in the App.

Data collected by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion policies of those services.

## 7. Children's privacy

WolfPass is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account registration system.

## 8. Security

WolfPass is designed to keep password information locally on the user's device and does not transmit stored passwords to the developer.

Users should keep their device, operating system, and App updated and should use appropriate device security measures.

## 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
